## (c) Risks and things not verified

**Alarming or decision-relevant**
- **Piper** (our hi/te TTS engine, GPL-3.0) says "Looking for Maintainers"; v1.8.0 is current but the bus factor is real. Keep a second TTS option in the benchmark (Indic Parler-TTS is the only Telugu-scored alternative found; it is 0.9B F32).
- **Agent frameworks keep shipping security holes**: UFO 10 advisories (2 critical, 2026-06..09), Windows-MCP High (2026-05-14), browser-use Critical allowlist bypass (2025-05-04). Playwright MCP lists none. Borrow ideas, do not run their servers.
- **Telemetry defaults ON**: Windows-MCP, Windows-Use, Skyvern, chrome-devtools-mcp, Presenton. Unknown/not stated: agent-browser, Playwright CLI, LiteParse, Autoconsent. Read the code and block egress before any pilot.
- **Licences to keep out**: Skyvern and workflow-use AGPL-3.0; Sarvam-translate GPL-3.0; Bitwarden MCP GPL-3.0; OmniParser v2 `icon_detect` AGPL (v3 is MIT, see table); cua `cua-som` AGPL and Spaces FSL; Supertonic-3 OpenRAIL-M; Autoconsent MPL-2.0 is file-level copyleft (keep it unmodified).
- **Prior D21 reason to skip Presenton is now partly stale** (telemetry opt-out exists). Treat the old "Linux only" note as unverified.
- **Sign-in is the sharpest edge**: Fara1.5 itself stops at sign-in; Playwright MCP `--secrets` is "not a security feature"; browser-use admits snapshots can still hold secrets. Item 8 must be proven with a hostile fake login page before any real account.

**Not verified (so do not state as fact)**
- Everything about D50 fit is from published sizes; **no model or tool was run**. Vulkan behaviour of Qwen3.5 vision (open llama.cpp issue noted in `PC_AGENT_RESEARCH.md`) was not re-read.
- ToS/AUP of **Gamma, Canva, Pitch, Slidesgo** could not be read (HTTP 403/404 to our fetch tool). Free-tier credit amounts for Gamma and Canva are **not confirmed from vendor pages**; Beautiful.ai's free tier was not looked up. A human or the laptop browser must read them before any automation.
- Live leaderboards (OSWorld, ScreenSpot, Online-Mind2Web) did not render; only dated notices and model-card numbers are quoted. Benchmark numbers are vendor-reported and use different protocols.
- Telugu: no source compares IndicConformer and SraVaani (or Omnilingual) on the same Telugu set; our own figure is one voice note (86% word recall). Omnilingual's Telugu/Hindi coverage is not shown on the GitHub page. Smart Turn does not list Telugu.
- Whether Windows has OCR packs for `hi-IN`/`te-IN` (item 9), and whether LiteParse's Tesseract ships Hindi/Telugu data.
- Holo-3.1-4B scores and GGUF availability; PaddleOCR-VL-1.6 GGUF and Indic coverage; Fara1.5 9B/27B numbers (search snippet only).
- Agent S4, GroundGUI, Mano-P weights licences are not stated on the pages read.
- Stars/forks are page figures and drift daily; "last release" uses `releases.atom` timestamps (UTC).
